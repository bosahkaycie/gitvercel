# Polaris Integrated & GeoSolutions Limited (PIGL)
## Official Website & Content Management System — Feature Handover Guide

Welcome to your new website! This document is designed for company leadership, managers, and administrative staff. It explains all the features built into your website, what they do, and how they make managing PIGL’s online presence simple and effective—all in plain, non-technical language.

## 1. Executive Summary: What We Built

We designed and built a fast, modern corporate website and an easy-to-use Control Panel (Admin Dashboard) tailored specifically to PIGL's identity as a premier Nigerian engineering, geosolutions, and 3D reality capture company. 

The website does four key jobs:
1. **Presents PIGL Professionally to the World:** Introduces your capabilities to oil & gas operators, infrastructure developers, government regulators, and international partners.
2. **Generates Business Inquiries & Leads:** Allows clients to easily request project quotes, contact specific departments, and download your official Company Profile.
3. **Puts You in Complete Control:** Gives your team an administrative dashboard where you can change menus, upload new documents, update social media links, review inquiries, and publish announcements without needing to write code.
4. **Dominates Search Engines (Google):** Built from the ground up so search engines immediately connect the short name **"PIGL"** with the full legal name **"Polaris Integrated & GeoSolutions Limited"**, displaying the official logo and preview images in search results.

## 2. What Website Visitors Experience (Public Features)

### A. First Impressions & Homepage
- **Cinematic Video & Hero Carousel:** When visitors arrive, they see high-quality visuals of your actual field operations, survey vessels, and engineering teams, paired with clear statements of what PIGL stands for.
- **Lightning-Fast Loading:** We trimmed down image and video weights so pages open smoothly and immediately on both office computers and mobile phones, even on slower mobile networks.
- **Interactive Reality Capture Slider:** Visitors can drag an interactive before-and-after slider to see how PIGL turns physical industrial plants into high-precision 3D digital models (Digital Twins).

### B. Core Business Divisions & Specialist Services
The website clearly presents PIGL's four approved operating divisions:
1. **GeoSolutions & Engineering Services (GES):** Land & hydrographic surveying, geotechnical soil investigations, and 3D laser scanning.
2. **Metocean & Marine Solutions (MMS):** Ocean currents, weather monitoring, marine surveys, and offshore positioning.
3. **Inspection, Repair & Maintenance (IRM):** Non-destructive testing (NDT), asset integrity assurance, and pipeline maintenance.
4. **Integrated Marine & Vessel Support (IS):** Specialized marine crew transfer, security vessels, and offshore logistics.

Each division includes clear descriptions, real deliverables (what clients receive), equipment used, and standards adhered to.

### C. One-Click Official Company Profile Download
- Visitors on desktop or mobile can click the **Download Company Profile** button in the header or footer.
- A popup immediately presents your official corporate profile with file details (size, version) and allows them to download the PDF instantly (`PIGL-Company-Profile.pdf`).
- If you ever update the profile in your dashboard, the download link automatically updates everywhere on the website.

### D. Interactive Projects & Case Studies
- A clean portfolio where clients can browse PIGL's track record across pipelines, offshore terminals, and infrastructure projects.
- Filters allow visitors to view projects by category (e.g. Geotechnical, Marine, Reality Capture).

### E. Executive Leadership & Engineering Teams
- Highlights PIGL’s leadership and specialist staff with professional photos, qualifications, and roles, reinforcing trust and indigenous engineering capability.

### F. QHSE, Safety & Corporate Responsibility (CSR)
- **Safety First (QHSE):** Showcases your ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certifications, safety track record, and compliance with statutory Nigerian regulators (NUPRC, NIMASA, NOSDRA).
- **Community & Environment (CSR):** Highlights local content development, youth training programs, and environmental stewardship in host communities.

### G. Video & Media Center
- Dedicated media library showcasing video clips of real PIGL operations: offshore drilling barges, swamp pipeline construction, drone reality capture, and subsea profiling.

### H. Contact, Request-a-Quote & Inquiries
- A comprehensive contact page with an interactive quote builder where potential clients can select the exact service they need, attach project specifications or drawings, and submit their message.
- Notifications are instantly routed to your official corporate inbox.

### I. Vendor & Subcontractor Registration Portal
- A structured onboarding area where prospective suppliers, equipment vendors, and contractors can submit their company details, registration documents, and compliance forms to work with PIGL.

### J. Instant Site-Wide Search
- A search tool (magnifying glass icon) that lets visitors type any keyword (e.g., "CPT", "pipeline", "bathymetry", "laser scanning") and instantly discover relevant services, articles, and pages.

## 3. How the Navigation Menus Work

We built a versatile menu system with three distinct options:

1. **Standard Direct Links:** Regular navigation links that take the visitor straight to a page (e.g., *Home*, *Contact Us*, *Careers*).
2. **Ordinary Dropdown Menus:** Clean, compact vertical lists that appear when hovering or tapping a menu item. Ideal for grouping related pages like *Governance* (QHSE Standards, CSR, Policies).
3. **The Mega Menu (Retained & Enhanced):** A full-width panoramic menu that appears under *Services* and *About Us*, displaying all four divisions side-by-side with short summaries, popular services, and a featured callout image.
4. **Mobile Navigation Drawer:** On smartphones and tablets, the menu smoothly slides in from the right. Visitors can tap to expand or collapse dropdowns and divisions without losing their place.

## 4. The Administrative Dashboard (Your Control Center)

To access your Control Center, visit: **`https://polarisigl.com/admin`** and log in with your administrative credentials.

Here is what you can manage inside the dashboard:

### A. WordPress-Style Navigation Menu Manager
- **Easy 2-Column Setup:**
  - **Left Side ("Add Menu Items"):** Simply check the box next to any site page or operating division, or type a custom link, and click **"Add to Menu"**.
  - **Right Side ("Menu Structure"):** See all your menu items laid out in order.
- **One-Click Reordering:** Click the Up (▲) or Down (▼) arrows to change the order in which links appear.
- **Choose Menu Style:** For any menu item, you can select whether it behaves as a **Direct Link**, an **Ordinary Dropdown**, or a **Mega Menu**.
- **Add Sub-Links in Seconds:** For ordinary dropdowns, you can type sub-links or pick them from a dropdown of existing pages.
- **Instant Publishing:** An indicator lets you know if there are unsaved changes. Clicking **"Save Navigation Menu"** instantly updates the live website for all visitors.

### B. Company Profile Document Management
- **Upload & Replace:** Upload your latest official company profile PDF at any time.
- **Safe Replacement:** The website will never show a broken link; if you replace the file, it seamlessly switches visitors to the new version once the upload finishes.
- **Live Status:** Shows the current published file name, file size (in MB), and the date it was last updated.
- **Delete / Unpublish:** You can take down the document with one click if it needs revision, which gracefully hides the download button on the public site until a new file is uploaded.

### C. Social Media Links & Icons Manager
- **Add & Edit Platforms:** Manage links for LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), WhatsApp, and others.
- **Turn On/Off:** Toggle any social icon on or off without deleting the link.
- **Custom Ordering:** Reorder social icons so your primary platforms (like LinkedIn) appear first.

### D. Homepage Carousel & Video Banner Manager
- Add new hero slides, edit headline text, update call-to-action buttons, or upload background images and videos.
- Easily toggle slides on or off for special campaigns or milestones.

### E. Inquiries & Quotes Inbox
- Read customer messages, quotation requests, and contact inquiries directly inside the admin panel.
- Filter messages by date or department, and download attachments sent by clients.

### F. Vendor Applications Manager
- View vendor submissions, review company profiles, and inspect compliance documents submitted through the vendor portal.

### G. Newsletter Subscribers
- View the list of clients and stakeholders who subscribed to your newsletter, with a one-click button to export the list to Excel or CSV.

### H. Page Headers & Breadcrumbs Manager
- Customize the top background banners and dark tint level (opacity) for any specific page (e.g. About, Services, CSR, Careers) to ensure text is always legible against background photos.
